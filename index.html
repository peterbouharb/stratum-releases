What it watches

SNMP devices, syslog, rack sensors and probes — polled locally by the app itself, on your network.

Where your data lives

On disk, next to the app. Nothing is uploaded to run monitoring — the app works fully offline after install.

Installing

Download, run the installer for your OS, open the app. That's the whole setup — no server to stand up first.
